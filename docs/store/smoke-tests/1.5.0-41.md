# Smoke test: Taqwa 1.5.0 (41)

The owner runs this on the Galaxy S23 and the iPhone 12 before build 41 goes to the stores. 1.5.0 ships the
Makkah and Madinah adhans, the whole adhan on Android, an adhan from the phone, the Lock Screen widget naming the
next prayer, and Malaysia's 2027 refit. The watch comes in 1.6.0 (42).

The build is release-1.5.0 (local, not pushed), on main c868d274 (the weekly monitor's proof of 2 Nov 2026).
The whole gate passes after the Malaysia refit: 20,865 rows, 0 early, 0 late ends.
scripts/test.sh passed 2,188 Android host and 2,216 iOS tests, plus widgetcore 220, with 0 failures. Strings and the
store listings are clean and the site check passes (168 pages, 57 cities).

## Install
- **S23:** uninstall the Play version first: this APK is signed with the upload key, not Play's, so Android refuses
  it over the store build. That clears Taqwa's settings and downloads. Then install `~/Desktop/Taqwa-1.5.0-41.apk`.
- **iPhone 12:** the Lead installs it with devicectl, or open `iosApp/iosApp.xcodeproj` from the release-1.5.0
  worktree, pick the iPhone 12 and press Run. Don't use TestFlight on the work iPhone 12.

## 1. Basics (both)
- [ ] It opens on Prayer, the countdown runs, and the times match yesterday's build.
- [ ] The widgets still show the next prayer.
- [ ] Settings › About Taqwa reads 1.5.0.

## 2. The adhan voices (both)
- [ ] Settings › Notifications › Adhan: TAQWA'S ADHANS lists Original, Makkah, Madinah, Besim Azemi, Aaqib Azeez, in
  that order, with no "New" pill. FROM YOUR PHONE sits last.
- [ ] Play Makkah and Madinah: each starts on the muezzin's first words, with no crowd noise or click before them.
  Besim Azemi starts without the cough.
- [ ] **S23:** the Adhan row reads "The complete adhan" and the sheet has no footnote. **iPhone:** the 30-second
  footnote is there.

## 3. The whole adhan on Android (S23)
- [ ] Set one prayer's Adhan to Makkah and let it fire (or wait for the next prayer). It plays past 30 seconds, to the
  end.
- [ ] Swipe the notification away mid-adhan: the sound stops.
- [ ] A prayer left on Takbir still plays only the takbir.

## 4. An adhan from your phone (both)
- [ ] FROM YOUR PHONE › Choose a recording opens the phone's file picker. Pick an audio file: the card reads "Your
  recording" with its name and length, it is chosen as the voice, and its play button plays it whole.
- [ ] Let a prayer fire with it. **S23:** the notification plays the whole recording.
  **iPhone:** it plays the first 30 seconds, fading out at the end.
- [ ] Choose another replaces it. Remove clears it, and the voice goes back to Original.
- [ ] Optional: pick something that isn't audio (or cancel the picker): nothing changes, or a short note says it
  couldn't be used.

## 5. Lock Screen widget (iPhone)
- [ ] The first line names the next prayer with its time ("Maghrib · المغرب  17:52"), not "Maghrib in". The "then"
  line has the following prayer's time at its end.
- [ ] Settings › Widgets' Lock Screen picture matches.

## 6. Prayer times settings (both)
- [ ] Settings › Prayer times: the Show sunrise / Time format card is right under the top card, above ASR. About these
  times is still the Timetable card's last row.

## 7. Never early against your mosque (both)
- [ ] Put today's five times and sunrise beside your mosque's board or printed timetable. No prayer starts before the
  mosque's time, and sunrise and the end of eating are never after it.
