# Smoke test: Taqwa 1.3.0 (39)

The owner runs this on the Galaxy S23 and the iPhone 12 before build 39 goes to the stores. 1.3.0 ships everything
since 1.2.0 as soon as it can; the watch comes in the release after it.

The build is release-1.3.0 at dc34d2c0 or later (local, not pushed). The gate at c7bb13ca, after the last engine change,
is 20,685 rows and 4,396,006 place-days: 0 early, 0 late ends, 71 stamps fresh. scripts/test.sh on 7badd335 passed
2,104 Android host and 2,088 iOS tests, plus widgetcore 111/109, with 0 failures. Strings are clean and the site check
passes (164 pages). The final proof on the release head is in the build-39 ledger.

## Install
- **S23:** install `~/Desktop/Taqwa-1.3.0-39.apk` over the store build (same upload key), by file transfer or with
  `adb install -r`.
- **iPhone 12:** open `iosApp/iosApp.xcodeproj` from the release-1.3.0 worktree, pick the iPhone 12 and press Run, or
  ask the Lead to install it with devicectl as for build 38. Don't use TestFlight on the work iPhone 12.

## 1. Basics (both)
- [ ] It opens on Prayer, the countdown runs, and the times match yesterday's build (Maghrib may move by a minute or
  two in a cautious place, see section 8).
- [ ] The widgets still show the next prayer.
- [ ] Settings › About Taqwa reads 1.3.0.

## 2. Duha and Iqama (both)
- [ ] **Off by default:** Settings › Notifications has two new quiet rows, both off: Iqama reminder (under Remind
  me before) and Duha (under Tahajjud). Tahajjud and Morning and evening adhkar now work the same way: off, each is
  its title and switch alone; on, its subtitle and Sound row slide in.
- [ ] **Iqama on:** its subtitle reads "Minutes after the adhan, not your mosque's own time", with Sound and a note
  below. Each prayer row shows its minutes (15, 10, 10, 5, 10 by default). Tap a prayer: its sheet has IQAMA
  REMINDER rows, Off and 5 to 30 min, and Dhuhr's says there is none on Fridays (the khutbah comes first).
- [ ] **A real iqama:** with Iqama on before a prayer, the adhan comes as usual and then, the chosen minutes later,
  "Iqama reminder · N minutes after the adhan" with its own sound. Maghrib's 5 minutes is the quickest to wait for.
  Tapping it opens the app.
- [ ] **Iqama off:** the minutes leave the prayer rows, and no iqama comes after the next adhan.
- [ ] **Duha on:** its subtitle says when it rings. When offers "At its best time" (midway between sunrise and Dhuhr)
  and "Once its time is in" (50 minutes or more after sunrise), and Sound is there.
- [ ] **A real Duha:** the next morning it rings once, "The best time for Duha · until <time>" or "Duha time is in ·
  until <time>", whichever you picked. The time it quotes is ten minutes before noon.
- [ ] **Duha off:** nothing comes the next morning.
- [ ] S23 only: Settings › Apps › Taqwa › Notifications lists a Duha category and an Iqama reminder category of their
  own.
- [ ] Optional: with Remove animations (S23) or Reduce Motion (iPhone) on, the rows a switch reveals appear at once
  instead of sliding in.

## 3. The player and the list ends (both)
- [ ] Start a recitation so the player bar shows, then go to the Quran tab. Scroll to the end of the Surah list:
  An-Nas sits fully above the bar, with the usual space below it. Do the same on Juz (Juz 30), on Bookmarks, and on a
  search's last hit.
- [ ] Stop the recitation so the bar goes. The lists end exactly as in 1.2.0, with no extra space.
- Known and not in this build: after a trip into the reader, the Quran tab goes back to Surah even when Juz was picked.

## 4. iPhone: a whole-Quran download, and cancelling it (iPhone 12)
- [ ] Settings › Recitation, with a reciter you have not downloaded: "Download the whole Quran · <size>". Start it.
  The row counts the surahs with a progress line and a Cancel below.
- [ ] If the phone is short of room, it takes only what fits and says there is not enough room. It never fills the
  phone (no Storage Almost Full).
- [ ] Let a few surahs land, then tap Cancel. The row goes back to the offer. No surah stays on its Verifying chip,
  and the next launch doesn't say Taqwa closed unexpectedly.
- Delete that reciter afterwards if you don't want it on the work phone.

## 5. Qibla (both)
- [ ] Turn slowly onto the Qibla: the rim and the tick light up when you are within 5°, and they stay on until you
  are more than 7° off. Resting near the edge, they don't flicker or tick again and again.
- [ ] While the compass asks for calibration, the screen stays awake. Once it is settled, the screen sleeps as usual.
- Neither phone lacks a compass. The no-compass screen (the bearing and distance, with no needle) shows on the iOS
  simulator, which has none, so the Lead can shoot it there.

## 6. Widgets (both)
- [ ] **iPhone lock screen:** the round widget names the next prayer in full, in English and in Arabic (الفجر, not
  الفج), and its ring isn't empty between midnight and Fajr.
- [ ] **No 0:00:** at a prayer's time, the widget moves on to the next prayer instead of showing "in 0:00".
- [ ] **To the minute:** the widgets' countdowns match the Prayer screen's minute.

## 7. Widget steps (S23)
- [ ] On the S23, Add widget (onboarding and Settings › Appearance) works as before: the S23's home screen accepts the
  widget, so no steps are shown.
- [ ] Only if you can make the home screen refuse: try with Settings › Home screen › Lock Home screen layout turned on.
  If Add widget then shows the steps by hand with Done instead, turn TalkBack on and tap Add widget again: TalkBack
  should read the steps. If it stays silent, swipe to the steps and check they read, and tell the Lead. If the S23
  still places the widget, skip this: the steps were checked on the emulator with a build that refuses.
- This comes from Bashir Ahmad's report (vivo V2310, Urdu), whose home screen doesn't take widgets from apps.

## 8. Onboarding at large text (S23, then the iPhone if you like)
- Do this last: onboarding comes back only on a fresh install. On the S23, Settings › Apps › Taqwa › Storage ›
  Clear data; on the iPhone, delete Taqwa and press Run again. Either one removes your settings and downloaded
  recitations. If you'd rather not, say so and the Lead shoots it on the emulator.
- [ ] At the default text size, go through onboarding: nothing is cut off, and the buttons stay at the foot.
- [ ] At the largest text (S23: Settings › Display › Font size and style, fully up; iPhone: Accessibility › Display
  & Text Size › Larger Text), the copy scrolls and Continue, Add widget, Done and Maybe later stay on screen.

## 9. The source label in a cautious city (both)
- [ ] In Cape Town (cautious times: the MJC's, the community calendar's and Jamiat's tables), a label in the top edge
  of the times card reads "Cautious times". About these times explains it and links Prayer times settings ›, now in the accent colour like Match my mosque ›.
- [ ] A city with a checked timetable (Istanbul, Makkah or London) shows no label. Dhaka shows "Published method" and
  Nairobi "Calculated by Taqwa".
- [ ] Optional: in Arabic the label reads «الأوقات الأحوط», in Urdu احتیاطی اوقات.

## 10. Maghrib in your own city (both)
- [ ] Compare today's Maghrib with every timetable the city's mosques follow. In Cape Town it is never before any of
  the three, and at most 2 minutes after the latest. It may differ slightly from 1.2.0's, since 1.3.0 drops the MJC
  margin that 1.2.0's cap needed.

## 11. Never early against your mosque (both)
- [ ] Put today's five times and sunrise beside your mosque's board or printed timetable. No prayer starts before the
  mosque's time, and sunrise and the end of eating are never after it.
