# Smoke test: Taqwa 1.5.1 (42)

The owner runs this on the Galaxy S23 and the iPhone 12 before build 42 goes to the stores. 1.5.1 is a patch on
1.5.0 (41): the Quran's small (superscript) alef is drawn in its place in the 322 words where the Hafs typeface put it
beside the word (1,333 occurrences, the basmala's ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ among them), and the Quran credit says truly what
Taqwa changes in Tanzil's text. Nothing else in the app changes. The watch comes in 1.6.0 (43).

The build is release-1.5.1 (local, not pushed): main bcafdfa9 (1.5.0 (41), the build on both stores) plus
fix/quran-31-11 at 99bbe66b, the credit and the version. The fix is a drawing choice only: nothing stored in
quran.db changes, and copy and share still hand out the stored text. Its report is
`docs/superpowers/reports/2026-11-07-quran-31-11.md`. The other differences from the print it lists (2:72 and the
18 words like it, 52:37, 12:11, 11:41, iqlab, the sukun's shape, staggered tanwin, lam-alef) are not fixed in 1.5.1,
so don't expect them to change.

## Install
- **S23:** before uninstalling, screenshot Mushaf pages 1, 351 and 411 in 1.5.0 for section 6. Then uninstall the
  Play version: this APK is signed with the upload key, not Play's, so Android refuses it over the store build. That
  clears Taqwa's settings and downloads. Then install `~/Desktop/Taqwa-1.5.1-42.apk`, which the real release.sh
  makes at the cut (it is not there until then).
- **iPhone 12:** open `iosApp/iosApp.xcodeproj` from the release-1.5.1 worktree, pick the iPhone 12 and press Run
  (or the Lead installs it with devicectl). Never use TestFlight on the work iPhone 12.

## 8. Strings for your native review
- The Quran credit (`credit_quran_text_detail`) in ar, bn, fr, id, tr and ur: `git diff build-41..release-1.5.1 --
  shared/src/commonMain/composeResources/values-<lang>/strings.xml` shows it beside the version.
- The site's Quran row in `site/pages/<lang>/home.html`, the same three changes, shorter.
- The tatweel is named as a drawn stroke, so no one reads it as a madd taken out: connecting stroke (tatweel),
  trait de liaison (tatwil), bağlantı çizgisi (tatvil), সংযোগ-রেখা (তাতওয়িল), garis sambung (tatwil). The Arabic
  says تطويل and the Urdu کشش; خط التطويل would be unmistakable in Arabic if you prefer it.
- The Play notes `docs/store/release-notes/1.5.1-42.txt` and the App Store notes
  `docs/store/release-notes/1.5.1-42-appstore.txt`, in seven languages, one item each. The Urdu, Bengali and
  Indonesian name the small alef by its school name (کھڑا زبر, খাড়া যবর, fathah berdiri), the Turkish and French
  describe it (küçük elif, petit alif suscrit). Turkish elif-ba calls it dik üstün or çeker, if you would rather
  the Turkish used the school name too.
- The App Review notes `docs/store/app-review/2026-11-07-notes-1.5.1-42.txt` (3,957 bytes of 4,000): the Quran-text
  line now names the licence and says the credit states the changes, and section 6 no longer says every
  provider's content is used under its published terms (Tanzil's notice forbids changes; whether Tanzil accepts
  these is still open).
- Before the release goes out: the draft note to Tanzil, `docs/superpowers/reports/2026-11-07-quran-31-11/tanzil-note.md`,
  is yours to send or not.
