# Smoke test: Taqwa 1.5.1 (42)

The owner runs this on the Galaxy S23 and the iPhone 12 before build 42 goes to the stores. 1.5.1 is a patch on
1.5.0 (41): the Quran is drawn with the 1405 AH Madinah Mushaf's own signs. The small (superscript) alef stands in
its place (31:11's ضَلَـٰلٍ, the basmala's ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ and every other word that has one); the sounded sukun is the
head of kha and the silent-letter sign the small ring; tanwin takes the Mushaf's staggered form, except before a
throat letter or ٱ, and a tanwin before the small meem of iqlab is one vowel and the meem; lam-alef carries its hamza
and madda; 43 of 44 words are written as the print writes them (2:72, 2:97, 52:37, the hamza below its seat in 14
words, and the line over the words of the 15 sajdahs), and 12:11 takes the King Fahd Complex's current solid dot; and
the sakta's small seen sits on its word. The two words that drew a dotted circle (12:11, 52:37) draw their sign. The
Quran credit says all of it. On Android two small changes come with it (section 12): an alarm whose notification
channel is missing makes it before posting, so an adhan armed by an older build is no longer dropped after an update,
and Report a problem adds two short lines. Nothing else in the app changes. The watch comes in 1.6.0 (43).

What is stored changed in one way only: quran.db (user_version 6) keeps Tanzil's own silent-letter sign (U+06DF)
again, so it is Tanzil's text byte for byte apart from the basmala, and copy and share hand out exactly that. Every
other change is in the drawing (`HafsSpelling.forDrawing`).

The build is the local branch release-1.5.1-marks: main feac1414 (the 1.5.1 (42) that was pushed and tagged but never
uploaded) plus the marks hotfix and the adhan fix (from fix/adhan-report-lines); the version is still 1.5.1 (42). The
tags build-42 and ios-build-42 still name feac1414; at the cut, with your OK, they move to the commit that ships. The
account is `docs/superpowers/reports/2026-11-07-quran-31-11.md` and its addendum of the same day. Mushaf pages and
lines below are the app's (page, then line from the top of the page, surah bands counted).

## Install
- **S23:** before uninstalling, screenshot Mushaf pages 1, 351 and 411 in 1.5.0 for section 10. Then uninstall the
  Play version: this APK is signed with the upload key, not Play's, so Android refuses it over the store build. That
  clears Taqwa's settings and downloads. Then install `~/Desktop/Taqwa-1.5.1-42.apk`, which the real release.sh
  makes at the cut (it is not there until then).
- **iPhone 12:** open `iosApp/iosApp.xcodeproj` from the release-1.5.1-marks worktree, pick the iPhone 12 and press
  Run (or the Lead installs it with devicectl). Never use TestFlight on the work iPhone 12.
- **The upgrade.** The iPhone goes on over 1.5.0, so it is the run that checks an update: the new quran.db (version 6)
  must replace the old one (section 4 shows it) and the ayah widget's old copy is refused (section 9). The S23 starts
  clean; on Android the same copy was checked on an emulator, installing this build over 1.3.0 (39).

## 2. The small alef: 31:11, the reader's word (both)

## 3. The basmala (both)
- [ ] Mushaf, page 411, the basmala above Luqman: in ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ the small alef stands above the word, with no tick
  on the line beside it; over the seen of بِسْمِ and the haa of ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ the sukun is the head of kha (a small open
  head like a ح without its tail), not a round ring. Page 1, Al-Fatiha 1:1: the same.
- [ ] Reader, the basmala above any surah but Al-Fatiha and At-Tawbah: the same.
- [ ] Prayer › Adhkar, the al-Ikhlas page (al-Falaq and an-Nas too): the basmala above the surah, the same.

## 4. The sukun and the silent-letter sign (both)
- [ ] Mushaf, page 2, line 7, Al-Baqarah 2:5: in أُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ the silent waw carries the small round ring; in رَّبِّهِمْ
  the final meem carries the head of kha. In 1.5.0 both were the ring. (On the iPhone, installed over 1.5.0, this
  also shows the update took the new database: with the old one the silent waw would carry the head of kha too.)
- [ ] Reader, 2:5: the same two signs.

## 5. Tanwin and iqlab (both)
- [ ] Mushaf, page 2, line 3, 2:2's هُدًى: its two fathas are staggered, one a little ahead of the other, not stacked.
- [ ] Mushaf, page 3, line 3, 2:7's عَذَابٌ عَظِيمٌ: عَذَابٌ, before the throat letter ع, keeps the stacked pair of
  dammas; عَظِيمٌ, at the ayah's end before 2:8's وَمِنَ, has them staggered.
- [ ] Mushaf, page 3, line 7, 2:10's أَلِيمٌۢ بِمَا: one damma and the small meem over the meem's letter, not two dammas

- [ ] Reader, Yunus 10:68 (page 216) and Ibrahim 14:3 (page 255): the low small meem of the iqlab words sits clear of
  the kasra, not run into it as in the first 1.5.1 cut.

## 6. Lam-alef with hamza and madda (both)
- [ ] Mushaf, page 2, line 6, 2:4's وَبِٱلْـَٔاخِرَةِ: the lam and alef are one lam-alef, with the hamza on the alef and
  a madda over it; no loose hamza on a stroke, no alef standing apart.

## 7. The words the print writes its own way (Reader and Mushaf)
- [ ] 2:72 فَٱدَّٰرَْٰٔتُمْ, page 11, line 5: after the raa's small alef a free hamza (ء), on no seat, with a head of kha
  over it, and a head of kha on the final meem; no blot over the raa. (The face draws that hamza a little raised, as
  the Complex's current print does; the 1405 print has it on the line. The reading is the same.)
- [ ] 2:97 لِّجِبْرِيلَ, page 15, line 7: the lam comes down and joins the jeem at the baseline, the jeem's head to its
  left; the jeem's bar does not cross back over the lam.
- [ ] 12:11 تَأْمَ۫نَّا, page 236, line 11: a solid dot over the meem, the ishmam sign, and no dotted circle. (The
  1405 print draws a hollow lozenge, which no Hafs font has; the dot is the King Fahd Complex's current form.)
- [ ] 52:37 ٱلْمُصَۣيْطِرُونَ, page 525, line 5: a small seen below the saad and the fatha above it, and no dotted circle.
- [ ] The hamza below its seat: 10:15 تِلْقَآئِ (page 210, line 3), 24:11 ٱمْرِئٍ (page 351, line 2), 56:23 ٱللُّؤْلُؤِ
  (page 535, line 3, on a waw): a bare tooth or waw with the hamza under it and the kasra (or the staggered
  kasratan) under the hamza, nothing printed over anything.
- [ ] The line over the words of a sajdah, a thin line over the word, clear of the row above: 7:206 يَسْجُدُونَ (page
  176, line 15); 17:107 يَخِرُّونَ لِلْأَذْقَانِ سُجَّدًا (page 293, line 4); 27:25 أَلَّا يَسْجُدُوا۟ (page 379, line 4);
  84:21 يَسْجُدُونَ (page 589, line 13); 96:19 وَٱسْجُدْ (page 597, line 15). The ۩ comes after the ayah at 7:206, 84:21
  and 96:19, and after a later ayah for the other two (17:109, 27:26). In the 1405 print the line at 27:25 also
  runs over لِلَّهِ and at 84:21 over لَا; the app's does not, as the Complex's current print does not, so do not look
  for it there (decided by Mohamed, 7 Nov 2026; see the addendum). The line may stop short between two overlined
  words, or run a little past a word at the line's start: glance at page 293 line 4, 309 line 8, 334 line 5, 365 line
  6, 416 line 7 and 454 line 11.
- [ ] 17:7 لِيَسُـۥٓـُٔوا۟, page 282, line 14: exactly as in 1.5.0: the small ring over the final alef, the small waw and
  the madda as before; no dotted circle.
- [ ] The sakta's small seen: 75:27 مَنْ ۜ رَاقٍ (page 578, line 3) and 83:14 بَلْ ۜ رَانَ (page 588, line 4): the seen
  sits over the word's last letter, not out over the gap or the start of the next word; 18:1 عِوَجَا ۜ (page 293, line
  12): over the alef, not out toward the ayah's roundel. In the Reader too.
