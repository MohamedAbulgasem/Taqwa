# Smoke test: Taqwa 1.2.0 (38)

The owner runs this on the Galaxy S23 and the iPhone 12 before build 38 goes to the stores. It is due by **21 October**:
- On phones with old zone data, Chihuahua goes an hour early on 25 October, and Syria and Jordan on 30 October.
- On iPhones, the EU clocks go back on 25 October, which is B7.

The build is release-1.1.0 at 186fea64 or later (local, not pushed). The whole gate is 20,627 rows: 0 early, 0 late ends,
nothing broken, stamps fresh. scripts/test.sh, with the budget assertion on, passed 1,972 Android host and 1,925 iOS
tests, plus widgetcore 99/97, with 0 failures. Strings are clean and the site check passes.

## Install
- **S23:** install `~/Desktop/Taqwa-1.2.0-38.apk` over the store build (same upload key), by file transfer or with
  `adb install -r`.
- **iPhone 12:** open `iosApp/iosApp.xcodeproj` from the site worktree (branch release-1.1.0), pick the iPhone 12 and
  press Run. Don't use TestFlight on the work iPhone 12.

## 1. Basics (both)
- [ ] It opens on Prayer. Cape Town's times match yesterday's build, and the countdown runs.
- [ ] The widgets still show the next prayer.
- [ ] Settings › About Taqwa reads 1.2.0.

## 2. iPhone: coming back to the app (B1, B19)
- [ ] **Notifications:** turn them off in iOS Settings › Taqwa, open Taqwa, then turn them on again in iOS Settings
  and come back. The next reminder should be set (Settings › Notifications shows no warning). One should fire at the
  next prayer without relaunching the app.
- [ ] **Control Center:** start a recitation, go to the Prayer tab, then pull down Control Center and let it go. You
  stay on Prayer. Do the same with Notification Center.
- [ ] **Return to the recitation:** with the recitation playing, press Home and come back through Now Playing (Lock
  Screen or Control Center). The reader opens on the recited ayah and follows it.
- [ ] **The app's own links:** with the recitation playing, go to Settings › Notifications and tap the link to iOS
  Settings, then come back. You are still on the Notifications screen.

## 3. Quran (both)
- [ ] **Play from where you read:** in translation mode, scroll into a surah and tap the header's Play. It starts
  there, not at ayah 1.
- [ ] **Open near the voice:** while Al-Kahf plays, open a bookmark or search hit on the page next to the recited one.
  The page stays where you opened it and doesn't jump to the voice.
- [ ] **Two surahs on one page:** in Mushaf mode on page 293 (Al-Isra ends, Al-Kahf begins), with Al-Kahf playing on
  that page, the header shows Pause and tapping it pauses.
- [ ] **Widget, twice:** tap the ayah widget, swipe a few pages on, go Home, then tap the same widget again. It
  reopens on the widget's ayah, highlighted.
- [ ] **Sheet across surahs:** play a short surah to its end with Aa (the reading sheet) open. The sheet comes back
  once the next surah has loaded.

## 4. Android notifications (S23)
- [ ] **Taps keep your place:** open the reader, then tap a prayer notification. The app comes forward still on the
  reader. This is NEW: before, it restarted onto Prayer.
- [ ] **Adhkar reminder:** an adhkar reminder's tap still opens its adhkar.
- [ ] **Media notification:** during a recitation, its tap opens the recited ayah.
- [ ] Optional: with notifications off, the widget still counts the next day.
  `adb shell dumpsys jobscheduler | grep -A3 taqwa` should show the top-up job.

## 5. Clock line (optional)
On current phones nothing should show. The line appears only on phones with outdated time zone data.

## 6. Listening checklist, accessibility round 1
Follow `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-28-taqwa-accessibility-round-1.md`, Task 12 Step 2b (line 1624). In short:
- **TalkBack (S23) and VoiceOver (iPhone):**
  - each switch says its name and on/off;
  - each picker says which option is selected;
  - the Prayer rows read as one sentence each;
  - the countdown reads as words;
  - the back button is named;
  - **the Quran's Arabic is never voiced**: ayahs read as "Ayah N" plus the translation.
- **iPhone only:**
  - VoiceOver's two-finger scrub goes back;
  - Voice Control's "Tap <name>" works on the 8 switch rows. If it fails, that's your ruling: a whole-row tap.
