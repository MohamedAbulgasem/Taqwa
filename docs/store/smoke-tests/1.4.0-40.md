# Smoke test: Taqwa 1.4.0 (40)

The owner runs this on the Galaxy S23 and the iPhone 12 before build 40 goes to the stores. 1.4.0 ships everything
since 1.3.0 except the watch, which comes in 1.5.0 (41).

The build is release-1.4.0 (local, not pushed), on main 5cc5a261 (the weekly monitor's proof of 1 Nov 2026). The
gate after the last engine change is 20,864 rows: 0 early, 0 late ends, 71 stamps fresh. scripts/test.sh passed
2,189 Android host and 2,215 iOS tests, plus widgetcore 220, with 0 failures. Strings are clean and the site check
passes (168 pages, 57 cities, 0 held).

## Install
- **S23:** uninstall the Play version first: this APK is signed with the upload key, not Play's, so Android refuses
  it over the store build. That clears Taqwa's settings and downloads. Then install `~/Desktop/Taqwa-1.4.0-40.apk`.
- **iPhone 12:** the Lead installs it with devicectl, or open `iosApp/iosApp.xcodeproj` from the release-1.4.0
  worktree, pick the iPhone 12 and press Run. Don't use TestFlight on the work iPhone 12.

## 1. Basics (both)
- [ ] It opens on Prayer, the countdown runs, and the times match yesterday's build.
- [ ] The widgets still show the next prayer.
- [ ] Settings › About Taqwa reads 1.4.0.

## 2. Notifications, prayers first (both)
- [ ] The master switch sits alone at the top. Turn it off: nothing shows below it. Turn it back on.
- [ ] PRAYERS: the five prayer rows. Below them, one card: Remind me before, Adhan, Iqama reminder.
- [ ] VOLUNTARY: one card with Tahajjud, Duha, and Morning and evening adhkar, each a quiet row until turned on.
- [ ] Turn Iqama on and off: the five rows gain and lose their iqama line, and the Iqama row stays under your finger.
  Try it once scrolled to the very bottom too.
- [ ] Optional: in French the label reads ACTES SURÉROGATOIRES, in Arabic النوافل والأذكار.

## 3. The main Settings screen (both)
- [ ] The App section has Appearance and Widgets. There is no Language row.
- [ ] The Appearance row reads your theme and language, e.g. "System · English" (in Arabic «حسب النظام · العربية»).
- [ ] About Taqwa sits alone in the About section.

## 4. Appearance and the language (both)
- [ ] Settings › Appearance: Theme, then a LANGUAGE section with your language's name.
- [ ] Tap it: the S23 opens Taqwa's app-language page, the iPhone Taqwa's page in the Settings app. Pick another
  language, come back, and the app shows it. Put your language back.

## 5. Settings › Widgets
- [ ] **Both:** the background choice is at the top, then each widget with its preview.
- [ ] **S23:** a widget already on the home screen shows a check. One that isn't shows an Add button: tap it, and the
  launcher's own dialog places it.
- [ ] **iPhone:** the first widget not yet on your Home Screen (Prayer times, if it is missing) shows the steps to add
  it by hand; the other widget card and the Lock Screen card ("Next prayer at a glance") each have a "Show me how" row
  that opens theirs. In the widget gallery the prayer widget is called
  "Prayer times", as the steps say. The Lock Screen picture reads "Maghrib in" and "then Isha", like the real
  accessory.
- [ ] **iPhone, VoiceOver:** turn VoiceOver on and open Settings › Widgets. Each widget and its steps are read out, and
  nothing is skipped or read twice. Turn it off again.

## 6. About Taqwa (both)
- [ ] The icon, Taqwa and 1.4.0, then Rate Taqwa and Share Taqwa, then the privacy promises with their marks, then
  open source and help.
- [ ] Rate opens the store page. Share opens the share sheet with Taqwa's link.
- [ ] At the largest text size, the Rate and Share pills stack instead of being cut off.

## 7. Prayer times settings (both)
- [ ] Settings › Prayer times: About these times is right below Manual adjustments, in the first card, and opens as
  before.

## 8. Downloads after the safety fixes
- [ ] **iPhone:** download one surah in Settings › Recitation and play it.
- [ ] **S23:** play a recitation and use the Bluetooth headset's media controls (or your Galaxy Watch's, if you wear it)
  to pause, skip and resume. They work as before.

## 9. Never early against your mosque (both)
- [ ] Put today's five times and sunrise beside your mosque's board or printed timetable. No prayer starts before the
  mosque's time, and sunrise and the end of eating are never after it.
- Optional, Cape Town: on the MJC's own timetable (Match my mosque), Asr is now a minute or two after the MJC's
  printed Asr, never before it.
