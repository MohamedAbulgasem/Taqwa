# Smoke test: Taqwa 1.7.0 (46)

The owner runs this on the Galaxy S23 (with his Galaxy Watch 8 Classic) and the iPhone 12 before build 46 goes to
the stores. 1.7.0 is three things on top of 1.6.0 (45):

- **The prayer times.** The never-early engine's work since 1.6.0: many more places are compared with their
  published timetables. Checked timetables (no label) now cover more of Türkiye (its il centres as 84 Diyanet units
  and the districts beside them), Egypt's 78 ESA towns, Morocco's 187 Habous places (checked through 30 November),
  all 60 of Malaysia's e-solat zones, 228 more Indonesian kab/kota, Kyrgyzstan's localities and Oman's 86 MARA
  localities. Places that keep the "Published method" label are compared on many more days: most of Algeria's
  wilayas, Bangladesh's 64 districts, Dawat-e-Islami's city cards in Pakistan and Kazakhstan's localities.
  Tajikistan and Côte d'Ivoire stay "Calculated by Taqwa", now held on the safe side of their national bodies, so
  their Dhuhr comes much later than in 1.6.0. A location found by the phone near a border can be held to both
  countries' timetables, and the far north's short summer nights are told as they are. Every gate row is 0 early
  (`docs/BUILD-LOG.md`, the entries since 12 November).
- **The Ayah of the day widget** draws from 365 ayahs, one for each day of a year (the hundred first, then 265 in
  Mushaf order); on Android the pool moved to a file of its own.
- **Home's watch card (Android only):** below the prayer list, above Qibla, while Settings › Watch offers "Install on
  your watch" or "Add the Taqwa face"; its x retires it for good.

The watch apps did not change; the Galaxy watch app and the face are rebuilt at 1.7.0 (100046) and (46) because one
release is four builds at one version (STORE-CHECKLIST §4).

The build is the local branch `release-1.7.0`: origin/main plus one squash commit of `engine/ramadan-final` (R60),
version 1.7.0 (46). Nothing is pushed until the stores approve and you say so.

## Install
- **Before you update:** screenshot the Prayer screen in 1.6.0 on the S23 (Cape Town, as it stands), for section 1.
- **S23 and the Galaxy Watch, from Play's internal testing (as for 1.6.0 (44) and (45), STORE-CHECKLIST's
  internal-track step):** put the phone AAB (46) on the phone's internal testing track and the Wear AAB (100046) on
  Wear OS's (the Releases page's form-factor selector), each with its notes file. Then update Taqwa from Play on the
  S23 and on the watch. Both come signed by Play, so the phone and the watch see each other as in the store; a
  phone APK signed with the upload key would not reach a Play-installed watch app, and section 5 would fail for that
  reason alone. This is an update over 1.6.0, so your settings stay.
- **iPhone 12:** open `iosApp/iosApp.xcodeproj` from the `release-1.7.0` worktree, pick the iPhone 12 and press Run
  (or the Lead installs it with devicectl). It goes on over 1.6.0, so it is the run that checks an update. Never use
  TestFlight on the work iPhone 12.

## 2. Prayer times elsewhere (both, about five minutes)
Settings › Location › search each city, look at Prayer, then open "About these times" from the times card. The label
is the small word above the card; no label means a checked timetable.
- [ ] İstanbul: no label; About names Diyanet's timetable.
- [ ] Cairo: no label; the Egyptian Survey Authority's timetable (checked through 31 December; from 1 January it
  reads "method, not yet fully checked" until a build carries 2027's table, by design).
- [ ] Casablanca: no label; Habous's timetable (checked through 30 November; from 1 December "Habous method, not yet
  fully checked" until a build carries December's table, by design, R275).
- [ ] Algiers: no label; the Ministry of Religious Affairs' timetable.
- [ ] Kuala Lumpur: no label; JAKIM's timetable (zone WLY01).
- [ ] Jakarta: no label; Kemenag's timetable.
- [ ] Bishkek: no label, new in 1.7.0 (it read "Published method" in 1.6.0); the Kyrgyz Muftiate's timetable.
- [ ] Muscat: no label; Oman's Awqaf timetable.
- [ ] Dhaka: "Published method"; the Islamic Foundation's method, and About says on how many days it was checked.
- [ ] Karachi: "Published method"; the Karachi method. Asr and Maghrib 2 minutes later than in 1.6.0.
- [ ] Almaty: "Published method"; the Kazakh Muftiate's method.
- [ ] Dushanbe: "Calculated by Taqwa", now on the safe side of Tajikistan's Council of Ulama: Dhuhr about half an
  hour later than in 1.6.0, by design (R207).
- [ ] Abidjan: "Calculated by Taqwa", now on the safe side of COSIM: Dhuhr most of an hour later than in 1.6.0, by
  design (R208).
- [ ] Tromsø: "Published method"; IRN's method. The times show, and About says what it says today (short nights in
  May–July are declared there).
- [ ] Back to your own location (Automatic) at the end; Cape Town's times as in section 1.

## 5. Home's watch card (S23 with the Galaxy Watch; Android only)
With Taqwa on the watch and the Taqwa face in use, Prayer shows no watch card. To see it (the reinstall first, since it
also gives the watch back its one face activation):
- [ ] Uninstall Taqwa from the watch, open Prayer: "Taqwa for your watch" with "Install on your watch". Tap it: the
  Play Store opens on the watch and the card says "Continue on your watch". Install it, then tap Open in the watch's
  Play Store (a reinstall publishes its face only once the app starts): within 3 minutes the card becomes "Add the
  Taqwa face" in place.
- [ ] Tap "Add the Taqwa face": the face screen opens on the watch; once the face is taken, the card folds. (Removing
  the face in the watch's own picker does not bring the card back by itself: the watch tells the phone only when
  Taqwa's Today opens, and not at all once its one face activation is used.)
- [ ] With the watch's Bluetooth off, tap the card: "Couldn't reach your watch" on two lines with "Try again"
  visible (your choice of 21 November).
- [ ] Press x, then Undo: the card comes back. Press x and wait 6 s: the line "Set up your watch any time in
  Settings." folds; after a relaunch the card is gone for good, and Settings › Watch still shows its rows.
- [ ] Phone in Arabic: the card's words as you approved them on 21 November.
- [ ] iPhone: no watch card anywhere (Android only in 1.7.0).
