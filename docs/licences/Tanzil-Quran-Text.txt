Tanzil Quran Text, Version 1.1 (Uthmani and Simple Clean)
https://tanzil.net

Taqwa's quran.db holds two Tanzil texts: the Uthmani text (ayah.text_uthmani, and the Mushaf's
page_line.text and line_word.text) and the Simple Clean text (ayah.text_search). Both are
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
on the terms of use in Tanzil's notice below, which must travel with every copy of the text.
The two notices are copied byte for byte from the end of the files tools/build-quran-db.py
downloads; quran.db carries the same two in its `notice` table.
