Used for: the Arabic text and its arrangement, the English translations and the transliterations of the 19 morning-and-evening items in `files/adhkar`; the recordings are not used.

Changes made (CC BY 4.0, section 3(a)(1)(B)): the 24 morning-and-evening entries are
reshaped into Taqwa's own schema and 19 of them are kept; the three Quls of Hisn 76 become
three items, and no Quranic text is taken from OpenDua (the app reads the Quran from its
own Tanzil text, by reference); eight transliteration typos are corrected, and the
quotation marks and the spelling "Allaah" are removed from the English of Hisn 96. The
Arabic is OpenDua's, unchanged. Every change is listed in tools/adhkar/README.md.
