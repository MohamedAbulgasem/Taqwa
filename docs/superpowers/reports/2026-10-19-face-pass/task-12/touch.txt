What the code reading said, and why it does not hold here
- Review round 1 read the system UI's dex (hhl, hhd, PositionLayout) and concluded that its hover
  dispatch gives a touch to the topmost item under the finger, so that slot 2's dates would answer
  almost anywhere and a touch would have found the prayer before Task 12. Neither was heard: the
  hover never reaches those items, because the system UI's views over the face take it first, and
  the items lie in the other order.
- Ruling 40 (the Lead, 24 Oct, option (b)) declared slot 2 before slot 0 (7, 2, 0, 1, 3, 6, 4, 5),
  so that the prayer's box would be the later of the two that lie across the face, for a touch;
  the swipe order became the time, the dates, the prayer. It was built in fix round 3 (fef65dc7),
  and nothing drawn changed (reorder-pass.txt).
- Ruling 42 (the Lead, 24 Oct) withdrew it, for option (a): a touch cannot be judged on this
  emulator, where no box or order of the face's own changes what a touch finds, and the swipe
  order is what a TalkBack user gets for certain; on a prayer face the prayer comes first. Slot
  2's block went back after slot 1's, unchanged (07e511be): without its comments the face is
  0298756d's again, byte for byte, so it draws as review round 3's pass of 0298756d did.

For plan 5's device pass
- A watch's system UI is its maker's (Samsung's on his Galaxy Watch 8 Classic), so a touch is
  checked there, with TalkBack and with Samsung's screen reader.
- If a touch does reach the face's items there, it is heard which of the prayer and the dates
  answers where their boxes overlap. If it is the dates, declaring slot 2 before slot 0 (Ruling
  40's option (b)) may be reopened with that evidence (Ruling 42): one reorder of slot 2's block,
  SourceManifestTest's read order and the docs. The limits are these. A ComplicationSlot is a child
  of the Scene only (formats 2 to 5) and has one bounding shape, so its box is the same in all four
  styles, and slots 0 and 2 still draw across the face from inside their boxes (the ring, the arch,
  the Sun's lit arc, the Moon's curves, the date lines and the Moon's curved Hijri date). And no
  two boxes may be the same inside the face (spec 3.6). Which item a touch finds where boxes
  overlap is to be heard there, not read from code.
