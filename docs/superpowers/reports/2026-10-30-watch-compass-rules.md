## What is new

- **`QiblaAngles`**: the angle rules above, for the phone and both watches.
- **`QiblaGuidance`** (`Turn`, `Guidance`, `QiblaGuidance.of`, `QiblaGuidanceTracker` with `reset()`): accessibility round 2's Task 9 Step 1 code, public and in `world.taqwa.app.qibla`, so round 2 uses this one. Round 2's own plan still shows it `internal` in `feature.qibla`; its Task 9 takes this one (the execution map's `a11y-round-2` line).
- **`WatchQibla`** (`DialState`, `DialStep`): `QiblaViewModel.collectWhileActive`'s body over a bearing. The gate first, then the filter on every sample; Low and BestEffort clear the alignment; Good is Aligned by `QiblaAngles.isAligned(smoothed, bearing, wasAligned)` and buzzes on entry only, otherwise Searching with the tracker's turn from `angleDelta(smoothed, bearing)`; every state but Searching gives the tracker null; `reset()` resets the filter, the gate, the tracker and the alignment. `NoSensor` is never answered by `onReading`: the screen chooses it before any reading (Task 7).

## After review

Four fix rounds. Rounds 1, 2 and 4 each fixed one Important from the code lens, the spec lens clean each time; in round 3 both lenses found the same Important.

- **Round 1 (`9568e251`), test only.** The tracker's 1 500 ms was pinned only from a take at t=0, so a tracker that never stamped `spokenAt` passed every case. `theIntervalRunsFromTheLastTakeNotFromZero` and `aHeadingWobblingAcrossTheBackIsSaidOnceInTheInterval` hold it; that mutant fails exactly those two.
- **Round 2: a NaN heading.** `QiblaGuidance.of` rounds with `roundToInt()`, which throws on NaN, and `WatchQibla` reached it on any trusted NaN heading. `HeadingFilter` keeps a NaN once it has one (the phone's behaviour, moved unchanged), so every later trusted reading threw until `reset()`, where the phone's view model shows Searching at NaN. Now `QiblaGuidanceTracker.update` takes a delta that is not finite as it takes null: no direction, and what was said is cleared. `of`'s KDoc says its delta must be finite. A NaN heading on the watch is therefore Searching at NaN with no turn, the same kind and heading as the phone. Red: the three new cases failed with `IllegalArgumentException: Cannot round NaN value.` and nothing else did. A guard on NaN alone (`isNaN()`) is killed by the infinities in `aDeltaThatIsNotANumberIsNoDirectionAsNullIs`.
- **Round 3, test only.** Nothing held how `WatchQibla` feeds the tracker while it searches: every `WatchQiblaTest` case on the turn was a first take after a fresh or cleared tracker, or a turn held, and the parity test compares no guidance. So the tracker fed `0L` in place of `reading.timestampMillis` (the turn told never changing for a whole spell of searching) and the tracker bypassed with `QiblaGuidance.of` on each reading (no 1 500 ms, no margin) both passed every qibla test in `:watchcore` and `:shared`. `searchingTellsANewTurnOnceInOneAndAHalfSecondsAndOnlyPastItsMargin` holds one spell on a boot clock from 10 000 ms with a filter that does not smooth: "right 45" from 340, held at 300 until 11 450 and "right 90" at 11 500; then at 3.35, "right 90" held until 12 950 and "right 15" at 13 000, the interval running from the second telling; then 3 s at 358.35 (25°, which alone rounds to 30) holding "right 15" within the margin. It passed on the code as it was; the `0L` mutant fails it at 11 500 ms, the bypass at the first reading at 300, and a tracker without its margin at 358.35. `WatchQibla.kt` and `QiblaGuidance.kt` are unchanged.
- **Round 4, test only.** The tracker's margin was held only for right turns: every margin case had a positive delta, and every left-turn case was a first take or a change of turn. So `abs(delta - current.degrees)` in place of `abs(abs(delta) - current.degrees)` passed every qibla test in `:watchcore` and `:shared`, and with the Qibla to the left it would speak every rounding change 1 500 ms after the last, the wobble round 2's rule keeps quiet. Three mirrors with negative deltas: `theTrackerHoldsALeftTurnAgainstAWobblingHandAsItDoesARightOne` (round 2's sequence from `update(0, -31.0)` to `update(3_300, -33.0)`), `aLeftStepMovesOnlyMoreThanTenAndAHalfDegreesFromTheOneItSaysToo` (-40.5 holds "left 30", -40.6 takes "left 45"), and `searchingTellsALeftTurnByTheSameClockAndMarginAsARightOne` (round 3's run mirrored about the bearing: 66.7, 106.7, 43.35, 48.35), so the vectors a port is held to carry a left turn too. They passed on the code as it was; the inner-abs mutant fails exactly those three of `:watchcore`'s host tests and nothing else (at 3 200 ms, at -40.5, and at 48.35). `WatchQibla.kt` and `QiblaGuidance.kt` are unchanged.
