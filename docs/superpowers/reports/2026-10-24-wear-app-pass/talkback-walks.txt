What TalkBack 16 for Wear said on emulator-5558, 31 October 2026 (plan 3 Task 9 Step 3), read from
its own log (tools/watchface/talkback.sh, its Diagnosis mode on). The watch's language is English;
the feeds are the debug build's samples at 14:58 on Sunday 18 October in Cape Town. Each walk line is
one "next item" swipe (one finger, left to right, through the emulator's touch screen) and what was
said for it, its fragments joined by "|", without TalkBack's "Double-tap to activate" hints. An empty
line is the end of the list, after which TalkBack starts again at the first item it holds.

- "opening" is what TalkBack said within 5 s of the page being opened with TalkBack on. "Home screen"
  and "Prayer times" are the face's (TalkBack turned on over the face, before the app opened), and
  "Taqwa" is the app's window.
- "idle" counts what was said with nothing touched: 10 s in the middle of a minute, then 10 s
  across the turn of a minute (the ring's sentence, which changes once a minute, is read again once).
- tap2 is TalkBack's double tap; "stored" is the count the app saved (shared_prefs/tasbeeh.xml),
  which shows a tap2 counted where its words fell outside the script's 3 s window.
- actions draws "Swipe down then right", which opens the TalkBack menu with "Actions" first on the
  counter; its tap2 lists the counter's actions, "Take one away" then "Choose a dhikr".
- "the pager, turned with TalkBack on" swipes with adb's input, which reaches the app and not
  TalkBack, as a sighted wearer's swipe would: it shows what TalkBack says at each change of page
  (the page's pane title). TalkBack's own way between pages is a two-finger swipe, which the
  harness cannot draw.
- Not kept: an en12 Tasbeeh run whose actions menu opened late, so that its taps landed on "Read
  from top" and TalkBack's continuous-reading tutorial (never confirmed; the tutorial's box stayed
  as found), and an ar-sa Tasbeeh run whose script checked for the English "Take one away". Both
  were run again, below.
