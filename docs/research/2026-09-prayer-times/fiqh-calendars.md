# Fiqh briefing (fiqh-calendars): how Sunni scholars treat calendars, official timetables and apps

**Scope:** This was read-only research. Your request to take down the 51 city pages was not carried out here; the parent session owns that change. The latest commit (ac9f53a, "every city page held") suggests it may already be done. **Please confirm.**

Confidence marks: **HIGH** means I read the primary page in this session. **MEDIUM** means it comes from a reliable secondary quotation, or the primary was not retrieved. **LOW** means one weak secondary source. Fatwa texts are paraphrased. Short Arabic quotes are used only for classical, public-domain works, plus one modern phrase.

## Bottom line
1. **Official timetables are the default.** A layperson should follow the local official or authority-approved timetable. An app is valid only when it reproduces those times, which supports the owner's rule. (Jordan 3610, Egypt 8409 and 2706, Ibn Baz 15636 and 15630, Mufti WP Bayan al-Falak 5.) **HIGH**
2. **When trusted sources disagree:** take the later time for a prayer's start, and the earlier time for the end of suhur. (islamqa 311727, islamweb 178295, Ibn Uthaymeen in Jalasat 1412, Mufti WP Irsyad 859.) **HIGH**
3. **Precaution must be small and have a physical cause** (rounding, the width of a time zone, elevation). Extra delay "for piety" is condemned classically (Ibn Hajar) and in modern fatwas (Ibn Uthaymeen, Banuri). **HIGH**
4. **Mistakes in the past:** most scholars say a prayer proven early must be repeated. There are real counter-positions for good-faith taqlid and for ijtihad replaced by a newer ijtihad. **HIGH**

## (a) Saudi scholars on the Umm al-Qura calendar
- **Ibn Uthaymeen, Liqa' al-Bab al-Maftuh no. 132** ([shamela 7687/3776](https://shamela.ws/book/7687/3776)). This is the exact source of the "five minutes" remark: Umm al-Qura is early "فيه تقديم خمس دقائق بالنسبة للفجر فقط". The other prayers differ by only one or two minutes. He says muezzins must delay the Fajr adhan by 5 minutes: being 5 minutes late does no harm, but even one minute early invalidates the adhan. **HIGH**
- **Ibn Uthaymeen, Majmu' Fatawa wa Rasa'il 19/302-303** ([shamela 12293/7574](https://shamela.ws/book/12293/7574)). Umm al-Qura is 5 minutes early at Fajr only. The claim of a third of an hour is an exaggeration. He rebukes muezzins who call Fajr early "for the sake of fasting", because that exposes people to praying before the time and forbids eating that Allah allowed. His point is that real precaution means following the Sharia, not the stricter option. **HIGH**
- **Ibn Uthaymeen, Jalasat Ramadaniyya 1412 [1]** ([shamela 7679/293](https://shamela.ws/book/7679/293)). Umm al-Qura was 5 minutes ahead of two other calendars (the "Bilal clock" and one made at the Petroleum University). The Muslim World League calendar was 3 minutes ahead. His rule: when trustworthy calculators differ, take the **latest**, because night is presumed to continue and taking the latest satisfies all of them. He also says calendars are used only out of necessity, like astronomical calculation for the crescent. **HIGH**
- **Ibn Uthaymeen, Ta'liqat 'ala al-Kafi 3/160** ([shamela 96875/1160](https://shamela.ws/book/96875/1160)). Calendars are early at Fajr by "at least five minutes". **HIGH**
- **His earlier, stronger view: Sharh Riyad al-Salihin 3/216.** Calendars are at least 5 minutes early, and people observing in the desert found about 20 minutes. He advised waiting 20 to 25 minutes before the Fajr iqama. This is quoted identically by [islamweb 193695](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/193695/) and [al-Ar'ur, shamela 147098/6-7](https://shamela.ws/book/147098/7). [Midad](https://midad.com/article/221380) calls it the older view. **MEDIUM** (I did not read the primary)
- **Ibn Baz, Nur 'ala al-Darb** ([binbaz 13203](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/13203/x)). Umm al-Qura is known and sound; someone out of earshot of any adhan may pray by it. **HIGH**
- **Ibn Baz, Fatawa al-Jami' al-Kabir** ([binbaz 1620](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/1620/x)). Calendar times are probabilistic (ẓannī) ijtihad. In his and others' experience they can be 5 or 10 minutes early at Fajr, or slightly late. A trustworthy direct observation takes precedence. **HIGH**
- **Ibn Baz on praying earlier than the calendar** ([binbaz 15636](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/15636/x)). Nobody may pray earlier than the calendar time. One must keep to the committee-made calendar unless one actually sees that it is wrong, as in the desert. **HIGH**
- **Ibn Baz on a Fajr adhan 1h40 before sunrise in Syria** ([binbaz 12549](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/12549/x)). He calls that adhan early and says delay is safer. **HIGH**
- **Ibn Baz's 1418 AH statement confirming Umm al-Qura** is reported by Grand Mufti Al al-Sheikh ([islamqa 66891](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/66891); [islamweb 138714](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/138714/)). I did not find the text of the statement itself. **MEDIUM**
- **Permanent Committee fatwa no. 4100.** A calendar is human ijtihad that can err, so the start and end of prayer and fasting should not be tied to it. Muezzins may use it as an approximation. Quoted identically by [islamweb 97824](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/97824/), [islamweb 217063](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/217063/) and [erej.org](https://erej.org/). **MEDIUM** (I did not retrieve the alifta primary page)
- **Al-Fawzan** ([al-fatawa 99000](https://al-fatawa.com/fatwa/99000/), citing alfawzan.af.org.sa node 16214). Umm al-Qura is authorised by the authorities and circulated to mosques, so it is a valid basis. **MEDIUM**

## (b) Fatwas on apps and computed timetables
- **Dar al-Ifta al-Misriyya no. 8409** (14 Jul 2024, Shawqi Allam; [link](https://www.dar-alifta.org/ar/fatwa/details/21036/x)). Smart watches and apps are permitted only if specialists have approved them and they match the user's country's official times; otherwise not. An app is only a means of revealing the official times. **HIGH**
- **Dar al-Ifta al-Misriyya no. 2706** (18 May 2013; [link](https://www.dar-alifta.org/ar/fatwa/details/13254/x)). Following the Egyptian Survey Authority calendar is obligatory. In that case al-Mahalla had to use its own earlier time, not Cairo's radio adhan. **HIGH**
- **Dar al-Ifta al-Misriyya no. 4021** (20 Mar 2017; [link](https://www.dar-alifta.org/ar/fatwa/details/13816/x)). The 19.5° Fajr is "definitely correct". The 14.7° proposal is rejected. **HIGH**
- **Jordan Dar al-Ifta no. 3610** (3 May 2021; [link](https://aliftaa.jo/fatwa/3610/)). Where apps differ from official times, follow the official times, especially for the start and end of the fast. Apps are acceptable only where no adhan can be heard. **HIGH**
- **islamweb 217063** ([link](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/217063/)). Addressed to someone building a prayer-time app: set the alert after the calendar time, because calendars are approximate. **HIGH**
- **islamweb 126606 and 109539.** Calendars approved by trustworthy scholars may be relied on. Calendars of unknown origin are only a rough aid. **HIGH**
- **islamqa 311727** ([link](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/311727)). When timetables differ, start the fast at the earlier Fajr and pray at the later one; take the later time for Maghrib. Using two Fajr times is fine. **HIGH**
- **islamqa 221219** ([link](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/221219)). An app 5 minutes earlier than the local mosque: follow the mosque if it keeps the city timetable, but watch the app for suhur. Stopping eating a little early is the lesser mistake. It quotes Ibn Baz, Majmu' 15/286. **HIGH** (Ibn Baz quote: MEDIUM)
- **islamqa 66891.** Only timetables made by qualified people **for that specific town** are valid; Qunfudhah may not use Makkah's times. **HIGH**
- **Ibn Baz on the radio adhan** ([binbaz 12953](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/12953/x)). A broadcast adhan can be relied on only if the broadcaster has proven truthful and careful; everything rests on trust. This is directly analogous to an app. **HIGH**
- **Malaysia (JAKIM framework, via Mufti WP):**
  - [Bayan al-Falak 5](https://www.muftiwp.gov.my/ms/artikel/bayan-al-falak/5088-bayan-al-falak-siri-ke-5-isu-ketepatan-waktu-solat-dalam-takwim-waktu-solat-pejabat-mufti) (7 Jan 2022): errors come from private or foreign timetables using different formulas and parameters from the state authority. **HIGH**
  - [Irsyad Hukum 859](https://www.muftiwp.gov.my/en/artikel/irsyad-fatwa/irsyad-fatwa-umum-cat/5965-irsyad-hukum-siri-ke-859-waktu-solat-di-masjid-masjid-berdekatan-berbeza-bolehkah-berpegang-dengan-azan-yang-paling-awal) (29 Jul 2024): when you cannot tell which adhan is reliable, follow the latest one. **HIGH**
- **Banuri Town no. 144109200208** ([link](https://www.banuri.edu.pk/readquestion/ghuroob-e-aaftab-k-bd-iftar-me-takheer-krna-144109200208/25-04-2020)), on Muslim Pro. Follow calendars certified by recognised institutions. They cannot vouch for the app's standard. **HIGH**
- **Deoband Fatwa 1367/1370/M=1433** (read via the [islamqa.org mirror](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/darulifta-deoband/78539/); the Deoband site is behind Cloudflare). Stop sehri 5 minutes early as a precaution. **MEDIUM**
- **Askimam #36045** (via [islamqa.org mirror](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/103380/should-we-keep-fast-and-pray-isha-according-to-15-degrees-or-18-degrees/)). The most cautious approach is 18° for the end of suhur and 15° for praying Fajr. **MEDIUM**
- **Diyanet on third-party apps:** not found.

## (c) "The imam is responsible and the muezzin is entrusted" (Abu Dawud 517, Tirmidhi 207)
- **Awn al-Ma'bud 2/163**, under Abu Dawud's chapter "ما يجب على المؤذن من تعاهد الوقت" ([islamweb library](https://islamweb.net/ar/library/index.php?page=bookcontents&ID=176&idfrom=860&idto=861&flag=0&bk_no=55&ayano=0&bookhad=0)). Citing al-Nihaya: the muezzin is "أمين الناس على صلاتهم وصيامهم". Citing al-Tibi: people rely on muezzins for all timed duties. The prayer "واغفر للمؤذنين" is explained as covering lapses "من جهة تقديم على الوقت أو تأخير عنه سهوا". **HIGH**
- **Tuhfat al-Ahwadhi 1/523** ([shamela 21662/521](https://shamela.ws/book/21662/521)). Same gloss. It prefers the meaning "trustee over prayer times", supported by al-Tabarani's "المؤذنون أمناء الله على فطرهم وسحورهم", which al-Haythami grades as having a hasan chain. **HIGH**
- **Ibn Majah 712** ("two things hang on the muezzins' necks") is quoted in Awn, but al-Albani reportedly graded it fabricated. **MEDIUM**
- **Grading of the main hadith as sahih:** not verified here. **MEDIUM**
- **Inference:** whoever publishes the times, including an app, stands in the muezzin's place of trust.

## (d) If an official or common timetable proves early
- **Prayers:** a prayer proven to be before its time must be repeated, whether one relied on a calendar or not. Sources: [islamweb 178295](https://www.islamweb.org/ar/fatwa/178295/) and [98083](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/98083/); Ibn Uthaymeen in Nur 'ala al-Darb via [islamweb 399712](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/399712/) (even one minute early does not count); [islamqa ar 527257](https://islamqa.info/ar/answers/527257). **HIGH**
  - The same islamqa answer notes the minority view (Ibn Taymiyya, Majmu' 21/101) that good-faith ignorance removes the need to make up prayers, but calls repeating safer. **HIGH**
  - A claim of early prayer needs proof; suspicion alone is not enough ([islamqa 103186](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/103186)). That answer quotes Ibn Uthaymeen (Fatawa vol. 12, q.146): no sin on people who trusted someone knowledgeable, yet waiting 5 to 15 minutes is better than praying one minute early. **HIGH** for islamqa's text, **MEDIUM** for the Ibn Uthaymeen citation
- **Malaysia, 2019:** when Subuh moved from 20° to 18° (+8 minutes), the Federal Territory fatwa committee ruled on 2 Dec 2019 that past Subuh prayers need no qada ([Bayan Linnas Khas, 6 Dec 2019](https://www.muftiwp.gov.my/en/artikel/bayan-linnas/3858-bayan-linnas-khas-isu-tambahan-waktu-8-minit-bagi-permulaan-azan-subuh-dan-hukum-solat-subuh-sebelum-masuk-waktu)). Reason: the old time was accepted ijtihad, and "الاجتهاد لا ينقض بمثله". **HIGH**
  - The same text cites al-Barizi, via al-Ramli: someone who erred about dawn for 20 years makes up only one prayer. **MEDIUM**
- **Fasts:** if you ate after true dawn believing it was still night:
  - Most scholars require a make-up day.
  - Mujahid, al-Hasan, one report from Ahmad, al-Muzani, Ibn Taymiyya (Majmu' 20/572 and 25/231) and Ibn Uthaymeen (al-Sharh al-Mumti' 6/402-411) say no make-up is needed ([islamqa 38543](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/38543), [312045](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/312045)). 312045 applies this to someone who used the wrong Fajr time after moving to Turkey. **HIGH**
  - Breaking the fast before sunset: the majority require a make-up day (al-Mughni 4/389; Permanent Committee 10/288; Ibn Baz Majmu' 15/288, all via [islamqa 66155](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/66155)). **MEDIUM**

## (e) Precaution minutes (tamkin / ihtiyat): how many, and why
- **Diyanet** ([temkin page](https://vakithesaplama.diyanet.gov.tr/temkin.php)). **HIGH**
  - Why temkin exists: a single time is published for a whole settlement that spans east to west and low to high ground. Too much temkin makes people pray before or after the time.
  - A 1982 High Board decision (in force from 1983) reduced it to: sunrise −7, Maghrib +7, Asr +4, Dhuhr +5 after the zenith (temkin plus the disliked time).
  - Imsak and Isha get **zero**. Temkin at imsak would mean either starting the fast late or praying Fajr early.
- **JAKIM / Selangor Mufti** ([FAQ](https://www.muftiselangor.gov.my/soalan-lazim/)). **HIGH**
  - Subuh, Zuhur, Asar, Maghrib and Isyak are rounded **up** to the next minute; syuruk (sunrise) is rounded **down**.
  - A further 2 to 5 minutes of ihtiyati is then added. Their worked example: a moment 10 s past a minute becomes the next minute, then 5 minutes later.
  - Imsak exists because a time zone is several minutes wide.
- **Mufti WP (Malaysia):** the published timetable already contains ihtiyat. It also recommends starting the Subuh congregation at least 8 to 10 minutes after the adhan. **HIGH**
- **Ibn Hajar, Fath al-Bari, Bab Ta'jil al-Iftar** (4/199 in the Salafiyya print; 4/235 in [islamweb's edition](https://islamweb.net/ar/library/index.php?page=bookcontents&ID=1240&idfrom=3581&flag=0&bk_no=52&ayano=0&bookhad=0)). He condemns calling the second adhan about a third of an hour before dawn "للاحتياط", and calling Maghrib a degree after sunset "لتمكين الوقت زعموا". In the same chapter, Ibn Daqiq al-'Id is quoted linking the delay of iftar until the stars appear to the Shia. **HIGH**
- **Banuri Town 144109200208:** a one-minute margin (iftar a minute after the calendar, suhur stopped a minute before) is precaution, not delay; a five-minute margin is not right. **HIGH**
- **Ibn Uthaymeen and Ibn Baz:** 5 minutes after Umm al-Qura for the Fajr adhan, and waiting before the prayer itself, but no early imsak adhan. **HIGH**
- **Common reason across all of these:** a small, explained margin for things the calculation cannot see, never piety-driven padding. **HIGH**

## What this means for Taqwa (my reasoning, not a fatwa)
1. adhan2's rounding to the nearest minute can show a start up to 30 seconds early and a sunrise up to 30 seconds late. That is the opposite of JAKIM's practice (round starts up, sunrise down). **HIGH** for the practice; the consequence is my inference.
2. Fajr cuts both ways: one number cannot be later for prayer and earlier for suhur at the same time. The scholarly answers are either an exact match with the authority (Diyanet uses zero temkin here) or two separate times, an earlier imsak and a later prayer time.
3. Don't delay Maghrib beyond the authority's own offset. Extra padding is what Ibn Hajar condemns, and it moves towards the Shia practice.
4. For users' past prayers and fasts, the rulings above differ, so the app should refer users to their local mufti rather than tell them what to repeat.
