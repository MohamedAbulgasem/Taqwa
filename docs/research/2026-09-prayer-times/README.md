# Prayer-time research, 25–26 September 2026

Why the city pages came down, what the authorities actually do, and what Islamic law asks of a
prayer-time app. The design that follows from it is
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-taqwa-prayer-times-engine-design.md`; the official timetables
it was measured against are listed in `tools/timetables/official/MANIFEST.tsv`.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `audit.md` | The live pages and the app against official tables, signed; the high-latitude clamp |
| `authorities-arab-world-turkiye.md` | The 20 authorities of the Arab world and Türkiye: methods, margins, rounding, reference points, tested adjustments |
| `authorities-asia-africa-europe-americas.md` | How each dominant Sunni timetable outside the Arab world and Türkiye is made (37 countries), measured against the app |
| `fiqh-rulings.md` | The rulings: a prayer before its time, fasting at both ends, precaution and its limit, imsak, calendars and apps, Asr and Isha by school, high latitudes, Ja'fari timing |
| `apps-and-failures.md` | What other prayer apps do, documented failures, certification, lessons |
| `profiles-tested.md` | Methods rebuilt from years of official data and tested on days and places they were not fitted on: Umm al-Qura, Diyanet, Kemenag, Egypt, Dubai, Qatar, the Maghreb, MUIS, JAKIM, Jamiatul Ulama |
| `fiqh-calendars.md` | Scholars and fatwa bodies on official calendars, apps, precaution minutes and past mistakes |
| `fiqh-fajr-angle.md` | The Fajr-angle debate and what laypeople are told to do |
| `fiqh-asr-isha-highlat-shia.md` | Asr and Isha by school, the high-latitude resolutions, Ja'fari timing |

The Arab-world, fiqh and apps files are written up from reports delivered in the session; the
others are the agents' own files. Findings are marked HIGH (primary source read), MEDIUM (reliable
secondary source) or LOW throughout; every figure about the app is app minus official, so a
negative number is the app showing a time early.
