# What Islamic law asks of a prayer-time app (research of 25–26 September 2026)

Written up from the round's four fiqh reports, which were delivered in the session (timing
mistakes; precaution minutes and responsibility; official calendars and the Fajr angle; Asr, Isha,
high latitudes and Ja'fari timing). The workflow's three follow-up briefings are the separate
`fiqh-calendars.md`, `fiqh-fajr-angle.md` and `fiqh-asr-isha-highlat-shia.md`. Confidence as the
reports gave it: **HIGH** = the primary text read; **MEDIUM** = quoted by a recognised body or a
reliable secondary source; **LOW** = a snippet or unresolved. Page numbers follow the edition
each site hosts (islamweb's library, shamela.ws). Quotations are kept to a line.

## 1. A prayer before its time

- Qur'an 4:103: prayer is "a decree of specified times" (HIGH). The hadith of Jibril (Abu Dawud
  393, Tirmidhi 149–150) defines each start and ties Maghrib to breaking the fast and Fajr to the
  start of the fast (HIGH).
- **All four schools: a prayer before its time does not count and must be repeated** (al-Mughni
  1/237–238; al-Majmu' 3/79; Kashshaf al-Qina' 1/315–318; HIGH). Contrary reports are marginal and
  concern someone genuinely mistaken: Ibn Abbas on a traveller's Zuhr before zawal, al-Hasan,
  al-Sha'bi, one report from Malik (al-Mughni; HIGH that he records them); Ibn Abd al-Barr judges
  them unauthentic and calls the rest agreement (MEDIUM, via islamweb 425254).
- Praying by a timetable or app that proves early: repeat it (al-Mughni 1/233; al-Majmu' 3/79; Ibn
  Baz fatwa 11014: about 10 min early, invalid, repeat; islamweb 133039: no sin under 33:5, repeat;
  HIGH). Ibn Uthaymeen: invalid "even by one minute" (islamweb 399712, HIGH).
- Made in good faith it counts as voluntary (al-Majmu' 3/249 for the Shafi'is, HIGH; Ibn Uthaymeen
  via islamweb 425254, MEDIUM).
- Praying while unsure the time has entered: wait until certain or it is most likely; delaying a
  little is recommended (al-Mughni 1/232–233; al-Iqna', via islamweb 258736; HIGH).

## 2. Fasting: sunset and dawn

- Asma' bint Abi Bakr, Bukhari 1959: "We broke our fast in the Prophet's time on a cloudy day, then
  the sun appeared"; Hisham: "بُدٌّ مِنْ قَضَاءٍ" (read as "is there any escape from making it up?");
  in Ma'mar's report he did not know (HIGH).
- **The four schools require a make-up day and no expiation** for breaking the fast before sunset,
  or eating after dawn, by mistake (Bada'i 2/100; Radd al-Muhtar 2/405–407; al-Dasuqi 1/526–527;
  al-Majmu' 6/326; al-Mughni 3/35: "this is the view of most scholars"; HIGH).
- **A respected minority says no make-up is due**: Ibn Taymiyya (Majmu' al-Fatawa 20/572–573,
  25/228–264, HIGH), Ishaq, Ahmad in one report, Ibn Khuzayma, al-Hasan, with Mujahid disputed
  (Musannaf Ibn Abi Shayba 9297 has him saying "he makes it up"; HIGH); Ibn Uthaymeen and Ibn
  al-Qayyim (MEDIUM). Umar's reports conflict both ways (Muwatta', Musannaf 9293–9304; HIGH).
- **Doubt is treated oppositely at the two ends.** At dawn one may eat while unsure ("night is
  presumed to continue"); at sunset one waits until sure ("day is presumed to continue")
  (al-Mughni 3/35; al-Majmu' 6/325–326; Ibn Baz fatwa 12023; HIGH). Ibn Abbas: "eat what you doubt
  until you no longer doubt" (al-Majmu' 6/325, "al-Bayhaqi, authentic chain"; HIGH).
- Hastening iftar applies only once sunset is established (Fath al-Bari, consensus quoted; HIGH).
- Eating during an early adhan does not affect the fast; the duty to stop begins at true dawn, not
  at hearing an adhan (islamweb 422614; Ibn Baz fatwa 1105; HIGH).

## 3. Precaution, and its limit

- **Ibn Hajar, Fath al-Bari (Kitab al-Sawm, bab ta'jil al-iftar; 4/199 Dar al-Ma'rifa):** among
  reprehensible innovations, the second adhan about a third of an hour before dawn in Ramadan, and
  calling Maghrib "a degree after sunset to make the time firm", so they "delayed iftar and hastened
  suhur and contradicted the Sunnah" (HIGH). A degree of the sun's motion is 4 minutes.
- Al-Shafi'i (al-Umm): delay is disliked only for someone who believes it virtuous (via Fath al-Bari
  and islamweb 187861; HIGH).
- **MWL Islamic Fiqh Council, 9th session, Res. 6 (12 Rajab 1406 / 23 Mar 1986):** Fajr 18°,
  sunrise and sunset at 50′, Isha at red twilight 17°; tamkin **+ 2 min on Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib and
  Isha, − 2 min on Fajr and sunrise** (HIGH, via the ECFR's full quotation).
- **Diyanet's temkin page:** one time per settlement has to hold for its east and west and its high
  and low ground; sunrise − 7, Dhuhr + 5 (also covering the makruh zenith), Asr + 4, Maghrib + 7,
  none on imsak or Isha; moderated by a 1982 decision applied from 1983 (HIGH).
- **Malaysia:** each zone's times are taken at its westernmost point, and a zone may span at most
  2 minutes east to west (Mufti of the Federal Territories FAQ; HIGH); the 2-minute limit was
  approved by most state muftis (UM paper, 2014; MEDIUM-HIGH). Subuh moved to 18° at the 116th
  Muzakarah of 20–21 Nov 2019 (Mufti WP Bayan Linnas 3858; HIGH); past Subuh prayers need not be
  repeated (Mufti WP committee, 2 Dec 2019).
- **Indonesia:** Kemenag's 2-minute ihtiyat since 1979, added to prayer times and subtracted from
  sunrise and imsak, seconds rounded up except sunrise (academic paper citing Depag 1994;
  MEDIUM-HIGH).
- Dhuhr: al-Hattab (via islamqa 370669): the Sharia moment comes "about half a degree after" the
  astronomers' (HIGH that it is quoted); al-Khathlan recommends + 3 min (MEDIUM).
- Delay the Fajr prayer a little after the calendar: islamweb 294715, 296551 (with Umm al-Qura);
  Ibn Uthaymeen via islamweb 126606 (stop eating at the calendar's Fajr, wait for the prayer until
  dawn is clear); the Federal Territories mufti (begin congregational Subuh 8–10 min after the adhan)
  (HIGH).

## 4. Imsak

- Against a fixed imsak: Ibn Uthaymeen ("an innovation… the Sunnah is against it", via islamqa
  12602; HIGH), Qaradawi (al-qaradawi.net 3732; HIGH), islamweb 261892 (HIGH).
- Tolerant of it as a voluntary precaution: islamweb 30009 ("imsak before the Fajr adhan is not an
  innovation"; HIGH), the Algerian Ministry (HIGH), the Mufti of the Federal Territories (Irsyad
  Ramadhan 76: imsak is a precaution, eating allowed until Subuh; HIGH).
- Zayd ibn Thabit's "fifty verses" between suhur and the adhan or prayer (Bukhari 1921, 575; Muslim
  1097; HIGH) is evidence for late suhur; using it for a buffer is an inference (MEDIUM).
- Where timetables disagree: start the fast at the earlier Fajr, pray at the later, break the fast at
  the later Maghrib (islamqa 311727; HIGH); stopping a little early is the lesser mistake (islamqa
  221219; HIGH).

## 5. Calendars, apps and whoever announces the time

- "The imam is a guarantor and the muezzin is entrusted" (Abu Dawud 517, Tirmidhi 207; al-Albani:
  sahih; HIGH, with the chain disputes Tirmidhi records).
- Ibn Baz: published timetables are probable, often 5–10 min off, observation by trustworthy people
  takes precedence (binbaz 1620); muezzins should not blindly follow calendars, "the people are in
  their charge" (binbaz 4885); a trustworthy muezzin may be relied on (binbaz 1105); no one may pray
  before the calendar time unless it is seen to be wrong (binbaz 15636) (HIGH).
- **Dar al-Ifta al-Misriyya 8409 (14 Jul 2024):** relying on apps and smart watches is permitted
  "when approved by specialists and calibrated to the timing of the user's country" (HIGH). Fatwa
  4021 (2017): the Survey Authority's 19.5° is "definitely correct and must be adopted" (HIGH).
- **Jordan Dar al-Ifta 3610 (2021):** where apps differ from official times, follow the official
  times; apps only where no adhan is heard (HIGH).
- islamweb 217063, to someone building a prayer app: "set the alert after the calendar time,
  because the calendar is approximate" (HIGH). islamqa 66891: a timetable must be by qualified
  people and **for the user's own town** (HIGH). islamweb 138714: do not carry Makkah's fixed
  intervals to another place (HIGH).
- Negligence in announcing the time is a sin; an honest error after effort is not, and those who
  broke their fast on it make it up in the majority view (islamweb 114527, 58218, 95520; HIGH).
  No fatwa names app developers; the rulings apply to whoever announces the time (inference).
- Umm al-Qura: endorsed by Ibn Baz (1418 AH) and the Grand Mufti (MEDIUM, via islamqa 66891);
  Ibn Uthaymeen judged its Fajr 5 min early and told muezzins to delay 5 min (Liqa' al-Bab al-Maftuh
  132; Majmu' 19/302; HIGH) — both statements predate its move from 19° to 18.5° in Dec 2008.

## 6. Incidents on record

- Malaysia, 3 Apr 2022: a radio station aired Maghrib 4 min early on 1 Ramadan; a former religious
  affairs minister and the Sabah mufti ruled fasts broken on it must be made up (Utusan; HIGH).
- Türkiye: Fatsa, 3 Apr 2023, akşam azan 5 min early, people broke their fast; Diyanet's fatwa board:
  iftar a few minutes (2–3) before the calendar time is covered by the temkin, earlier must be made
  up (kurul.diyanet.gov.tr; HIGH).
- Malaysia, 2021: printed mosque calendars 2–10 min off; the Federal Territories mufti forbids
  merged-day tables (Bayan al-Falak 26; HIGH).

## 7. Asr and Isha by school

- Asr: the majority, and Abu Yusuf and Muhammad, at one shadow length plus the noon shadow; Abu
  Hanifa at two (al-Hidaya 1/40; Bada'i 1/122–123; HIGH). Ibn Abidin (Radd 1/359): two lengths is the
  manifest narration and the fatwa position; the cautious course is not to delay Zuhr past one length
  and not to pray Asr before two (HIGH). Banuri Town: two lengths is the fatwa view; its Karachi
  timetable fits 18°/18° with Hanafi Asr (HIGH). Deoband: a prayer at one length is valid in need
  (HIGH). Diyanet: asr-ı evvel for the official İkindi, no reason stated (HIGH).
- Isha: red twilight for the majority, white for Abu Hanifa (al-Hidaya 1/40–41); in the late Hanafi
  texts the fatwa is on red, white being the cautious option (Radd 1/359, 1/361; HIGH). Angles: MWL
  Res. 6 puts red twilight at 17° (HIGH); Karachi's 18° corresponds to white.

## 8. High latitudes

- Saudi Council of Senior Scholars, Res. 61 (12/4/1398): real times however long the day while day
  and night are distinguishable; the nearest land only where the sun does not set or rise (HIGH).
- MWL Res. 6 (1986): 45°–48° real times; 48°–66° estimate the missing Isha and Fajr by proportion to
  45°; above 66° all times by proportion to 45° (HIGH via ECFR). ECFR 1/11 (2003) and 2/12 (2004)
  adopt it and also accept 12° or fixed 1.5 h, and combining Maghrib and Isha; 4/27 (2018) urges one
  unified timetable per country (HIGH, Wayback). The Dajjal hadith (Muslim 2937): "estimate its
  measure" (HIGH). No council text found endorsing one-seventh of the night or the middle of the night.
- Diyanet abroad since 1 Jan 2023: Isha 16°, the 2021 congress estimation north of 44.5° with a
  gradual transition (research files; HIGH that it exists).

## 9. Ja'fari timing (so that a Sunni app can label itself)

- Sistani, Islamic Laws 722: do not pray Maghrib until the eastern redness has passed overhead,
  as an obligatory precaution; 723/726: Maghrib and Isha until midnight (midpoint of sunset and
  dawn); 717: Zuhr and Asr share one span; fasting runs to that Maghrib; breaking the fast on a
  trusted report that proved wrong requires a make-up day (sistani.org; HIGH). No minutes are given;
  a 4.5° sun (Tehran method) is 18–21 min after sunset in Tehran and 24–33 in London (calculation).
- Sunni side: Abu Dawud 418 ("as long as they do not delay Maghrib until the stars crowd"), Bukhari
  561; delaying Maghrib that far is disliked (Radd 1/368–369) (HIGH).
