Prayer times for Tatarstan, 2026, from the Spiritual Administration of Muslims of the Republic of
Tatarstan (DUM RT), https://dumrt.ru/netcat_files/multifile/2649/vremena_namazov_RT_2026_0.xlsx,
licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), accessed 25 September 2026.
Converted to one line per day. `off_kzn.txt` carries Kazan's sahur-end column in the Fajr position
and the printed zenith; `off_kzn_mosque.txt` carries Kazan's separate "mosque Fajr" column
(sunrise − 91 min) and the fixed Zuhr the mosques keep (12:00). `dumrt-2026-<town>.txt` carries,
for each of the other 18 localities the app lists, every column of the table as printed: end of
sahur, mosque Fajr, sunrise, zenith, Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha (changed only in layout).
