## The Quran items (hisn-075, 076a, 076b, 076c)

- `tr.json` has no entry for them, and `check_items.py` would fail one (it requires exactly the 15 du‘a ids); the README says the app shows its own Quran translation for them. Nothing in `tr.json` translates the Quran.
- The page reads the reader's chosen translation from `quran.db` (`AdhkarViewModel.quranText`); a Turkish interface defaults to `tr.diyanet` (`ReadingSettings.kt`, `"tr" to "tr.diyanet"`). Each ayah's text is therefore Diyanet's, verbatim. 2:255 and 112:1–4 read correctly.
- **Defect, outside this file: al-Falaq and an-Nas repeat.** Tanzil's `tr.diyanet` stores a sentence that spans several ayahs on every one of them: 113:1–5 hold one identical sentence and 114:1–6 another (570 such runs across the whole translation; no other bundled translation has them, apart from the genuinely identical 94:5–6). `quranText` joins a whole surah's ayah texts with spaces, so a Turkish reader sees the al-Falaq sentence **5 times** in a row (974 characters) and the an-Nas sentence **6 times** (1,145 characters), on the page itself (and in what a screen reader voices of it). Joining each run of identical consecutive texts once would show Diyanet's text verbatim and once. This is in `AdhkarViewModel.kt`, not in `tr.json`, and does not depend on the `reviewed` flag (it follows the Quran translation setting, so it also reaches any interface whose reader picked Diyanet). Not fixed here.
